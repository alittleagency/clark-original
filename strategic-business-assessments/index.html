Assess & Plan

Clark & Associates helps effectively assess and plan your business change for a smooth implementation of SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, PeopleSoft, and Microsoft or other technology solutions. Our expertise includes CRM, Financial, Human Resources, Procurement, Asset Management, Business Intelligence, and other key business areas.

Core capabilities

System selections

Our system selection methodology evaluates all key areas before you make a purchase. Get the information you need to quickly develop an RFP, shortlist your vendors, and make the right selection while avoiding unnecessary costs and lengthy timelines.

Pre-implementation readiness assessments

We help you identify the key areas affected by your planned change, as well as potential issues. You gain the knowledge to resolve issues before the change or to develop a mitigation plan.

Business analysis and requirements gathering

Our approach identifies and prioritizes your requirements for a well-defined project scope, driving out costly gaps in process changes and system customizations.

RFI, RFQ, and proposal management

Already narrowed the field to a short list? We manage the RFP process. Our vendor-neutral approach gives you an apples-to-apples comparison.
